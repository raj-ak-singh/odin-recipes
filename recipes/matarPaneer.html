MATAR PANEER

home

Description

Matar Paneer is a classic North Indian dish featuring a flavorful medley of sweet green peas (matar) and soft paneer cheese.

These two main elements are simmered together in a rich, spiced tomato-based gravy that traditionally starts with a fragrant aromatic base of onions, ginger, and garlic.


The dish perfectly balances contrasting textures and rich flavors, offering a delicate sweetness from the fresh peas alongside the creamy mildness of the paneer.

Warmly spiced with staples like turmeric, garam masala, and chili, it is usually finished with a splash of cream and a garnish of fresh cilantro. It is most commonly served hot alongside flatbreads like roti, naan, or fluffy basmati rice

Ingredients

  • Paneer (cottage cheese cubes, usually 200g - 400g)
  • Green Peas (matar, fresh, thawed, or boiled)
  • Onions (finely chopped or blended into a paste)
  • Tomatoes (chopped, pureed, or canned)
  • Ginger & Garlic (minced or made into a paste)
  • Green Chilies for heat
  • Cashews (soaked and blended into a paste)
  • Oil or Ghee (or butter) for sautéing
  • Cumin seeds (jeera)
  • Bay leaf (tej patta)
  • Cardamom (elaichi), cinnamon, and cloves
  • Turmeric powder (haldi)
  • Kashmiri red chili powder or regular chili powder
  • Coriander powder (dhania)
  • Garam masala
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
  • Fresh coriander leaves (cilantro)

Steps

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil or ghee in a pan and sauté 1 chopped large onion, 1 inch of ginger, and 4 to 5 garlic cloves until soft.
  2. Add 2 large chopped tomatoes and 10 to 12 whole cashews, then cook until the tomatoes turn mushy.
  3. Let the mixture cool completely, then blend it with a splash of water into a smooth paste.
  4. Cut 200 grams of paneer into cubes. Optionally, lightly fry them and soak them in warm water for 10 minutes to keep them soft.
  5. Heat 1.5 tablespoons of oil or ghee in the same pan, then add 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds and 1 bay leaf until they sizzle.
  6. Lower the heat, pour in the blended onion-tomato-cashew paste, and stir well.
  7. Stir in 1/2 teaspoon of turmeric, 1 teaspoon of Kashmiri red chili powder, and 1 teaspoon of coriander powder.
  8. Sauté the mixture on low-medium heat for 5 to 7 minutes until the masala thickens and oil starts to separate from the sides.
  9. Add 1 cup of green peas, 1 to 1.5 cups of water, and salt to taste, then cover and cook for 5 minutes until the peas are tender.
  10. Gently drop in the paneer cubes and stir carefully so they do not break.
  11. Cover and let it simmer on low heat for another 3 to 4 minutes so the paneer absorbs the flavours.
  12. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon of garam masala and 1 tablespoon of crushed kasuri methi over the curry.
  13. Turn off the heat and stir in 2 tablespoons of fresh cream or malai for extra richness.
  14. Garnish the top with finely chopped fresh coriander leaves
